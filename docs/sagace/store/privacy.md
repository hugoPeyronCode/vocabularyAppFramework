# Sagace privacy policy

Last updated: {DATE}. Publisher: Cortex SASU, {ADDRESS}. Contact: {CONTACT_EMAIL}.

## In short
Sagace needs no account and collects no personal data. There are no ads and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

## What stays on your iPhone
- **Your progress**: cards seen and sorted, reviews, streaks, game and level test results, achievements.
- **Your library**: collections, words you added, liked words carried over from Words.
- **Your settings**: reminders, voice, appearance, daily goal.
- **Your error reports** and, in the beta, your reviews.

This data is stored in the app's space, shared with its widgets. It is never sent to us, and it is erased when you delete the app.

## Purchases
Purchases and subscriptions go through Apple. We never receive your name, contact details or payment method. The app only checks with Apple whether Sagace Premium is active.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**: reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved.
- **Photos**: the app only adds a card image you choose to save. It does not read your photo library.

## Pronunciation
Words are read aloud with iOS voices, on your iPhone.

## Error reports and reviews
An error report stays on your iPhone. In the beta, you can choose to send your reviews: you pick the way to send them (mail, message…) and the recipient yourself.

## Analytics
Version 4.0 sends no usage statistics. If anonymous analytics are added, this policy will be updated first.

## External links
Card sources (Wiktionary, Wikisource, Creative Commons…) open in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Children
Sagace is suitable for all ages and collects no data, including from children.

## Your rights
Since no personal data reaches us, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Questions: {CONTACT_EMAIL}.
